How to Purchase & Activate Your License

Only 4 simple steps to receive your premium digital products. Fully automated, instant, and 100% secure.

4 Easy Steps to Order

1

Select Your Product

Browse our digital catalog on the homepage. Click on any product to view license specifications, durations, and terms.

2

Enter Email & Checkout

Click Buy Now and enter your active email address. Your email is used to receive your product pickup code, receipt, and access details.

3

Pay via Real-time QRIS

Scan the generated QRIS barcode using your preferred banking or e-wallet app. Our system verifies incoming transactions automatically.

4

Instant License Delivery

Within seconds after payment confirmation, your license key/account credentials appear on-screen and a full copy is emailed to your inbox.

Page Closed or Need to Re-check?

Do not worry if your browser tab closes during or after payment. You can retrieve your license anytime using any of these 3 methods:

  • Get Product Page: Visit Get Product and enter your Pickup Code (e.g. CLM-XXXXXX) or Invoice Number (INV-XXXXXX).
  • My Orders: Click the My Orders button in the top navbar to view all transactions stored securely in your browser.
  • Check Email Inbox: Open the email you entered during checkout. A full receipt with your product pickup code and direct access link has been sent automatically.

Need Activation Assistance?

Certain software licenses require specific setup or account redemption procedures. Comprehensive guidelines are always included in your license delivery box.

24/7 Dedicated Customer Support

If you encounter any activation questions, our support team is ready on WhatsApp: WhatsApp CS. We will guide you until your license is active!

My Order History

Your order history is securely stored in this browser without requiring an account. You can access or re-claim your licenses anytime.
